Everly helps you remember things that recur at their own pace. This policy explains what happens to the data you enter.

💾 Where Your Data Is Stored

  • On your device. Habits, check-ins and notes are stored locally, shared only with the app's own widget.
  • In your iCloud. If iCloud is enabled, Apple syncs this data between your devices in your private iCloud database. Only you can access it; the developer cannot. Apple's handling is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Backups you export. An exported backup is a file you save where you choose. It stays under your control.

📱 Information We Collect

Everly has no account, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives your habits, notes, dates or any other content you enter.

To learn which features are used and to find bugs, Everly sends anonymous usage signals through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in the EU. A signal says, for example, "a habit was created, repeating every month, with a reminder", or "an export failed". It also carries the app and system version and a rough count of your habits (such as "5–9"). It never contains a habit's name, a note or a date.

Signals are not linked to you: TelemetryDeck receives only a random, hashed identifier, not your name, Apple ID, email, IP address or advertising identifier. The data is not used to track you across apps or websites, and it is not sold or shared.

👥 Use of TelemetryDeck to analyze app usage

We use the privacy-friendly analytics service TelemetryDeck (provider: TelemetryDeck GmbH, Von-der-Tann-Str. 54, 86159 Augsburg, Germany) to analyze usage data. The use is based on Art. 6 para. 1 lit. b GDPR, as we require reliable and efficient tools for collecting app usage data in order to fulfill the contract with you, our customer.

What data is transferred?

The data processed by TelemetryDeck is completely anonymized and does not allow any conclusions to be drawn about personal information. The following data is collected, among other things:

  • an anonymized, untraceable user ID (per app installation);
  • actions defined by us (e.g. "app launched," "settings opened");
  • a rounded timestamp (to the nearest hour);
  • device metadata (e.g. system version, app version, device type);
  • additional metadata defined by us (e.g. "number of items in the database").

What is not stored?

  • No IP addresses (not in logs, not in the database);
  • No cookies or tracking technologies;
  • No persistent identifiers that could be traced back to individuals.

The source code of the TelemetryDeck SDK is completely open source and available on GitHub: github.com/TelemetryDeck.

Further information on the exact data processing by TelemetryDeck can be found at: Privacy and Privacy FAQ.

The app does not use advertising or cross-app tracking services.

🔔 Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent through any server.

🗑️ Deleting Your Data

Delete a habit in the app, or delete the app. To remove the synced copy too, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage and delete Everly's data.

👶 Children

Everly does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. The anonymous usage signals described above are the same for every user.
