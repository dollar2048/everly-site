Everly

Everly reminds you of things that recur at their own pace: a pill twice a day, a dog's worming tablet every three months, a post every seven days.

Common Questions

  • Data not showing on another device: make sure both devices use the same Apple Account and iCloud is enabled for Everly.
  • Reminders not arriving: check Settings → Notifications → Everly.
  • Moving to a new device: iCloud syncs automatically; you can also export a backup in Settings.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. In short: your habits and notes never leave your devices and your iCloud; the app only sends anonymous usage counts.
